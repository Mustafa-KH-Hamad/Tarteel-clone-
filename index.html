      How it works

      Choose a surah, press the microphone and recite. Each word turns green when it is correct, red when it is wrong and orange when you skip it. You can go back and repeat earlier ayat at any time; that is not counted as a mistake. Tap any word to start from there.

      Speech is recognised by your browser's built-in speech service, so you need Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Safari, and an internet connection. In Brave, Firefox and other browsers without that service, the app automatically uses an on-device Quran speech model instead (a Whisper model trained on recitation by Tarteel AI). It downloads about 100 MB once, runs entirely on your device, and checks each phrase when you pause. The app checks words; it cannot judge tajweed, and it may sometimes mishear a word.
