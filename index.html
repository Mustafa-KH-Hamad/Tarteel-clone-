        How it works

        Choose a surah, press the microphone and recite. Each word turns green when it is correct, red when it is wrong and orange when you skip it. You can go back and repeat earlier ayat at any time; that is not counted as a mistake. Tap any word to start from there.

        Speech is recognised by your browser's built-in speech service, so you need Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Safari, and an internet connection. In Brave, Firefox and other browsers without that service, the app automatically uses an on-device Quran speech model instead. It downloads about 100 MB once and checks each phrase when you pause.

        Quran text: Uthmani script from The Noble Qur'an Encyclopedia (quranenc.com); English translation Saheeh International, via the open quran-json dataset (CC BY-SA 4.0). Kurdish translation (Burhan Muhammad Amin) via the open fawazahmed0/quran-api. Tafsir texts via the open spa5k/tafsir_api. Page layout of the Madani mushaf via quran-meta (MIT). Recitations from EveryAyah.com. The harakat & tajweed check uses the open Quran Muaalem model.

        AI notice: the speech recognition and the harakat & tajweed check are AI and may make mistakes. This app is an aid only and is not a substitute for a proficient, authorised Quran teacher — the Quran is received through oral transmission (mushafahah).
